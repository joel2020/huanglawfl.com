Accessibility

Huang Law is committed to improving access to its website and services.

Updated August 25, 2026

Our ongoing effort

Huang Law is working to make this website usable by people with a wide range of abilities and assistive technologies. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and the website may not yet work perfectly for every visitor, device, browser, or assistive technology.

As the site evolves, Huang Law intends to review content, structure, keyboard access, focus visibility, color contrast, images, forms, and responsive layouts and to address identified barriers when reasonably practicable.

Need assistance or want to report a barrier?

If you have difficulty using this website or accessing information, please contact Huang Law at 305-389-3339 or dan@huanglawfl.com. Please describe the page or feature involved and, if comfortable, the browser or assistive technology you were using.

Huang Law will make a reasonable effort to provide the requested information through an accessible alternative and to consider reported barriers as part of its ongoing improvement work.

Third-party content

Some links may lead to websites or services controlled by third parties. Huang Law cannot control or guarantee the accessibility of third-party content but welcomes notice when an external resource creates a barrier.
