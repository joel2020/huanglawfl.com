Practice Area

Commercial Collections

Creditor-side representation in unpaid commercial accounts and contract-based debt matters.

From demand through judgment

A commercial collection matter may begin with a demand and proceed through litigation. Huang Law represents creditors in commercial debt matters through these stages in Florida state and federal courts where admitted.

The appropriate approach depends on the governing documents, the parties, the amount and nature of the claimed obligation, available evidence, and the procedural posture of the matter.

Scope of representation

Depending on the engagement, commercial collection work may include:

  • reviewing contracts, account records, payment history, and other supporting documents;
  • pre-suit demands and communications concerning unpaid commercial obligations;
  • civil litigation involving commercial accounts or contract-based debt; and
  • coordination with appropriate judgment-enforcement work if payment does not follow a judgment.
