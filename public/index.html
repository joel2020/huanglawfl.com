Commercial Collections

From unpaid account to judgment

A commercial collection matter may begin with a demand and proceed through litigation. If payment still does not follow, the work shifts to locating nonexempt assets and selecting the proper enforcement remedy.

Huang Law represents creditors through these stages in Florida state and federal courts.
