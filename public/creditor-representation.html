Representation of creditors in bankruptcy proceedings and related civil litigation.

Creditor-side representation

Bankruptcy and related litigation can affect the timing, forum, and procedures available to a creditor. Huang Law represents creditors in bankruptcy proceedings and related civil litigation in the courts where Dan Huang is admitted.

The scope of an engagement depends on the nature of the claim, the pending proceeding, applicable deadlines, and the creditor's objectives.
